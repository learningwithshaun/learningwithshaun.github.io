# Agile Logbook — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](evidence.md)

## What I produced

I kept an Agile Logbook with a taskboard, a walkthrough of the flow of work, and stand-up entries answering the three standard questions: what I completed, what I plan next, and what is blocking me.

## What I learned

The logbook gives a running record of my placement. It connects directly to my monthly reports and makes it easy to see progress, such as moving from the landing page to Journey 1 and then to the Raspberry Pi. Recording impediments honestly, including learning WordPress and the Pi overheating, also made it clearer where I needed support.

## Challenges

My entries are weekly because that is the level of detail in my monthly reports. Many weeks say "None reported", which may hide small blockers that I did not record at the time.

## What I would do differently

I would log daily, record impediments when they happen, and attach evidence such as screenshots of the landing page and journey pages, so each entry is easier to verify.

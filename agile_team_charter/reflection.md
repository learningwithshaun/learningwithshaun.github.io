# Agile Team Charter — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](evidence.md)

## What I produced

I adapted the Agile Team Charter from last semester's team version into one that fits an industry placement. It sets out my role as developer intern, my mentor as Product Owner, our ceremonies (daily stand-up, weekly planning, review and retrospective), a Definition of Done, and the main risks and constraints.

## What I learned

A charter only works if it describes how work is really assigned and approved. At Vic3e my mentor sets priorities and signs off deliverables, so I changed the decision-making section to a Product Owner model. Writing the Definition of Done made me think about what "finished" means for each project: for the documentation site it means proofread, working links and tested on mobile; for Lulem it means tested on the TV and Raspberry Pi.

## Challenges

I am the only learner on the projects, so parts of the template written for a team of students, such as voting and consensus, did not apply directly. I also had to balance two projects and occasional events like the summit, which is why those appear as risks.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the charter with my mentor in the first week and have it signed then, rather than finalising it later. I would also review it at each retrospective so it stays accurate as my priorities change.

## 1. Team Purpose & Vision

**Team mission** — We help Vic3e's customers get value from its products. Zuke users need clear, step-by-step guidance, and retail businesses need a simple way to run advertising and take payments on in-store TVs. We deliver this through a documentation site (Zuke) and a digital-signage application (Lulem).

**Success definition** — Success looks like:
- A live Zuke documentation site where a new user can complete sign-up and other key tasks without help.
- Lulem running reliably on a Raspberry Pi connected to a retail TV, playing ads, showing Paystack QR codes and playing YouTube content between ad sessions.
- Work approved by the mentor and logged consistently.

**Product / service** — We are responsible for:
1. The Zuke documentation site (landing page and user journeys, built in WordPress with Elementor).
2. The Lulem digital-signage application and its Raspberry Pi setup.
3. Supporting marketing and media work (Lulem promo video, summit preparation).

## 3. Ways of Working

### 3.1 Agile Framework
- ☒ **Scrum-style, 1-week sprints** (aligned to weekly progress in the monthly reports)
- ☐ Kanban

### 3.2 Ceremonies

| Ceremony | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Stand-up | Daily (company stand-up with the team) | Share completed work, next steps and impediments |
| Sprint Planning | Start of each week | Pick backlog items for the week, clarify acceptance criteria with the mentor |
| Review / Demo | End of each week | Show completed work to the mentor and collect feedback |
| Retrospective | End of each week (15 min) | What went well, what didn't, one improvement to act on |

## 4. Working Agreements
- We arrive prepared and on time for stand-ups and ceremonies.
- We respect timeboxes (stand-up 15 min; planning 30 min; retrospective 15 min).
- We give specific, constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- We raise blockers early, no later than the next stand-up.
- We act on retrospective action items.
- We log work daily so the logbook stays accurate.

## 5. Communication
- **Primary channels:** company messaging channel for day-to-day coordination (WhatsApp/Slack/Teams — confirm); email for formal communication and lecturer correspondence; in-person meetings for stand-ups, reviews and demos.
- **Response times:** urgent blockers within 2 hours in core hours; non-urgent questions within 24 hours.
- **Core working hours:** Tuesday–Friday, 09:00–16:00 (Thursday 08:00–17:00), per the attendance register.
- **Remote / hybrid norms:** share screen when presenting work; camera on for virtual meetings; mute when not speaking.

## 6. Decision-Making
- ☐ Consensus
- ☒ **Product Owner / Mentor final decision**, with input from the team
- ☐ Team vote
- ☐ Agile Lead decides after input

**Escalation path:** If a design or technical decision isn't settled within one working day, the Mentor makes the final call. Issues that affect scope or academic requirements are escalated to the WIL lecturer.

## 7. Definition of Done (DoD)
- [x] Work matches the acceptance criteria agreed with the mentor
- [x] Reviewed by the mentor (or a team member)
- [x] Tested on the target setup (desktop and mobile for the site; TV and Raspberry Pi for Lulem)
- [x] Content proofread (spelling, links, screenshots, step order)
- [x] Published or deployed to the agreed environment
- [x] Documentation updated (user guide, logbook, backlog status)
- [x] Mentor sign-off received

## 8. Core Values & Behaviours
- **Respect** — Value everyone's time and expertise; listen actively.
- **Transparency** — Share progress honestly, including delays and mistakes.
- **Accountability** — Own your tasks and update the team without being chased.
- **Trust** — Assume positive intent and support each other.
- **Learning mindset** — Treat new tools (WordPress, Raspberry Pi, 3D printing) and feedback as chances to grow.

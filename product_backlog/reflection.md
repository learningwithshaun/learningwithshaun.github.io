# Product Backlog — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](evidence.md)

## What I produced

I built a Product Backlog of all 13 stories, with a MoSCoW priority, story points and a current status for each.

## What I learned

Prioritising made me separate what must be delivered (the documentation journeys, Lulem playback, payments and Pi setup) from what is valuable but flexible (the promo video, summit support, the fan case). Showing each item as To Do, In Progress or Done gives my mentor and me one view of where the work stands, and it shows how requests like the summit arrive mid-project and need to be absorbed.

## Challenges

Estimating points was difficult for unfamiliar work such as Raspberry Pi configuration and 3D printing, so those estimates are rough. Several Lulem items are still in progress, which shows how much was running in parallel.

## What I would do differently

I would re-estimate after each sprint and compare estimates to the actual effort, so the estimates get more reliable as I learn the tools.

# Sprint Backlog — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](evidence.md)

## What I produced

I organised my placement work into 10 weekly sprints, from August Week 1 to September Week 5, each with a goal, the backlog items and tasks worked on, and the outcome.

## What I learned

Mapping the weeks to sprint goals shows how the work moved: onboarding and WordPress, the landing page, the Lulem promo video, Journey 1, then the Raspberry Pi and the documentation site. It also shows unplanned work. Summit preparation took a full week, and the Raspberry Pi overheating led to the 3D-printed fan case in Week 3 of September.

## Challenges

Unplanned work interrupted the plan more than once, and the Zuke and Lulem work had to share my time. Because I wrote the sprint backlog from my reports, some goals were set after the fact rather than at the start of each week.

## What I would do differently

I would write the sprint goal at the start of each week and leave spare capacity for unplanned requests, so that changes are recorded as they happen.

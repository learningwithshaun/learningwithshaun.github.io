# User Stories and Tasks — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](evidence.md)

## What I produced

I wrote 13 user stories across four epics (Zuke Documentation Site, Lulem, Marketing and Media, and WIL Admin), each with acceptance criteria and tasks.

## What I learned

Writing from the user's point of view kept me focused on who the work is for: new Zuke customers who need a clear sign-up guide, and retail owners who want ads and payment QR codes on their TVs. Breaking the landing page into sketch, build, iterate and approve mirrored how I actually worked in August. Acceptance criteria also made it clear what mentor approval means for each piece of work.

## Challenges

Some work, such as configuring the Raspberry Pi and designing the fan case, does not fit the usual user-story format. I framed these with the developer as the user. Lulem was also assigned after I had started the documentation site, so its stories describe features of an existing product rather than something I planned from scratch.

## What I would do differently

I would write the stories and agree acceptance criteria with my mentor when a task is assigned, before starting work, instead of documenting them afterwards.
